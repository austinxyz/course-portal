把它抽象成"可复用引擎"、套到其他目标上的洞察由 Austin 提出。The insight of abstracting it into a "reusable engine" applicable to other goals was proposed by Austin.

很多同学知道 5C 是 Nan 的找工作方法论：Many of you know 5C as Nan's job search methodology:

C1 方向清晰 → C2 建立底气 → C3 建立人脉 → C4 高效转化 → C5 做出承诺C1 Clarity → C2 Credibility → C3 Community → C4 Conversion → C5 Commitment

但这几天我想明白一件事，正好可以帮你们找到 Demo Day 的灵感：5C 根本不是"找工作"专用的，它是一台通用引擎。But I've realized something that can help you find your Demo Day inspiration: 5C is not just for job hunting — it's a universal engine.

5C 的本质The Essence of 5C

把它抽象出来看，5C 其实是一个「目标驱动的 搜索 → 外联 → 转化」流程：Abstracting it, 5C is a goal-driven Search → Outreach → Conversion pipeline:

先想清楚要什么(C1)，把自己准备好(C2)，找到对的人(C3)，高效转化(C4)，然后坚持迭代(C5)。Get clear on what you want (C1), prepare yourself (C2), find the right people (C3), convert efficiently (C4), then iterate (C5).

而几乎所有"我想得到某个机会"的事，都是这个形状。你只要换掉「目标」，同一套引擎就能跑出完全不同的工作流：Almost every "I want to get this opportunity" situation has this shape. Just swap the target and the same engine produces a completely different workflow:

  • 目标是全职岗位 → 这就是原版找工作Target: full-time job → the original use case
  • 目标是付费演讲机会（有同学正好在愁这个）→ C1 筛会议、C3 找主办方、C4 定制 pitchTarget: paid speaking gig → C1 filter conferences, C3 find organizers, C4 custom pitch
  • 目标是客户（做保险、做咨询、接单）→ C1 锁定人群、C3 触达、C4 跟进成交Target: clients (insurance, consulting, freelance) → C1 define audience, C3 reach out, C4 follow up and close
  • 目标是学员 / 报名 → 招生本身就是一次 5CTarget: students / enrollments → recruiting is itself a 5C run
  • 目标是投资人、合作伙伴、供应商 → 同样成立Target: investors, partners, suppliers → same structure applies

引擎不变，变的只有四样：目标、去哪找（来源）、怎么打分（筛选标准）、说什么（话术）。The engine stays constant. Only four things change: target, where to find them (source), how to score them (criteria), and what to say (pitch).

而这正是 AI 的用武之地——发现、筛选、起草、跟进这些又累又碎的活，全可以交给 AI 自动跑；你只负责真正需要人的部分：建立关系、谈条件、最后拍板。And this is exactly where AI shines — discovery, filtering, drafting, follow-up, all the tedious repetitive work can run on autopilot. You handle what only humans can: building relationships, negotiating, making the final call.

这跟你的 Demo 有什么关系What Does This Mean for Your Demo?

Demo Day 不需要你做一个很炫的东西，需要的是一个真正解决你自己问题的工作流。而 5C 给了你一个现成的骨架——你不用从零想，只要回答四个问题：Demo Day doesn't need something flashy — it needs a workflow that genuinely solves your own problem. 5C gives you a ready-made skeleton. You don't have to start from scratch, just answer four questions:

  1. 目标Target：我最想得到的那个"机会"是什么？（一份工作 / 一批客户 / 一个演讲 / 一个合作）What is the "opportunity" I want most? (a job / a batch of clients / a speaking slot / a partnership)
  2. 来源Source：这种机会一般出现在哪里？（招聘网站 / 会议 CFP / 行业群 / LinkedIn / 协会名单）Where do these opportunities usually appear? (job boards / conference CFPs / industry groups / LinkedIn / association lists)
  3. 打分Scoring：什么样的算"值得投"？（给不给钱、契合度、地域、难度）What counts as "worth pursuing"? (compensation, fit, location, difficulty)
  4. 话术Pitch：我要对他们说什么？（为什么是我、为什么是现在）What do I say to them? (why me, why now)

把这四个变量填进 5C，让 AI 去跑发现、筛选、起草、跟进——这就是一个完整、可演示的 demo。Plug these four variables into 5C, let AI run the discovery, filtering, drafting, and follow-up — that's a complete, demo-ready workflow.

给你的思考题A Question for You

你自己最想要的那个"机会"，能不能也套进 5C？Can the opportunity you want most also fit into 5C?

把它写下来，定义清楚那四个变量。你会发现，你已经拥有了一个属于自己的、AI 驱动的工作流——而且它在 Demo Day 之后还能继续帮你赚到时间和钱。Write it down and define those four variables. You'll find you already have your own AI-driven workflow — one that keeps generating time and money well after Demo Day.

这，才是学会用 AI 的真正方式：不是记住一个模板，而是看懂底层结构，然后把它套到你自己的目标上。That is the real way to master AI: not memorizing a template, but understanding the underlying structure and applying it to your own goals.
