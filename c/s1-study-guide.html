第 1 步Step 1 · 记心得 + 让 AI 帮你总结Write Reflections + Let AI Summarize

先你写，再让 AI 补。Write first, then let AI fill in. 顺序很重要——AI 是来帮你深化的，不是替你回忆的。Order matters — AI is here to deepen your thinking, not recall it for you.

  1. 在 我的心得.md 里，尽量回忆课上听到的内容，记下你的体会。不用写得完善，碎片、半句话都行。In 我的心得.md, recall what you heard in class and write down your thoughts. No need to be thorough — fragments and half-sentences are fine.
  2. 在 不懂的名词.md 里，记下你没太懂的术语和概念。In 不懂的名词.md, note down any terms and concepts you didn't quite understand.
  3. 把 PPT 放进 ppt/ 目录；再把下载包里的 跟我做-提示词.md 也放进 课程心得/——它是课堂 demo 用到的 提示词清单，每步还标了「这步在干嘛」，AI 翻它能准确还原每步在做什么。Put the PPT in the ppt/ directory; also put 跟我做-提示词.md from the download package into 课程心得/ — it's the prompt list used in the class demo, with each step annotated with "what this step does", so AI can accurately reconstruct what each step is doing.
  4. 然后让 AI 结合你的心得、PPT 和 跟我做-提示词.md 帮你总结。多问几次，每次问得更细，最后要求它 输出一个 课程总结.md 文件，方便你以后回看。Then ask AI to summarize by combining your reflections, the PPT, and 跟我做-提示词.md. Ask multiple times, going deeper each time, and finally ask it to output a 课程总结.md file for future reference.
📎 跟我做-提示词.md 为什么重要Why 跟我做-提示词.md matters：PPT 是「讲了什么」，跟我做-提示词.md 是「实际发了哪些提示词」。 你对某一步没看懂、或想不起 demo 细节时，让 AI 翻 跟我做-提示词.md 回答你，比凭空解释准得多。The PPT covers "what was taught"; 跟我做-提示词.md covers "what prompts were actually sent". When you don't understand a step or can't remember demo details, asking AI to reference 跟我做-提示词.md gives much more accurate answers than explaining from scratch.
Prompt 示例Prompt Examples（直接抄，再改成你自己的）：(copy directly, then adapt to your own):
  • 「请结合我的心得（我的心得.md）、PPT（ppt/ 里的文件）和 跟我做-提示词.md，帮我总结这节课，保存到 课程总结.md。」Please combine my reflections (我的心得.md), the PPT (files in ppt/), and 跟我做-提示词.md to summarize this class for me, and save it to 课程总结.md."
  • 「我对 demo 里『___ 那一步』没看懂，请结合 跟我做-提示词.md 给我讲清楚那一步在干嘛、为什么这么做。」I didn't understand the '___' step in the demo. Please use 跟我做-提示词.md to explain clearly what that step does and why."
  • 「我对 cowork 里怎么配置 Gmail 不太懂，请给我更详细的步骤指导，并补充到总结文件里。」I'm not clear on how to configure Gmail in Cowork. Please give me more detailed step-by-step guidance and add it to the summary file."
  • 「对于『如何写好 prompt』我现在还没概念，请帮我系统梳理一下，加到总结里。」I have no idea yet about 'how to write good prompts'. Please help me systematically organize this and add it to the summary."
💡 技巧Tip：把「不懂的名词」逐个丢给 AI，让它用大白话 + 例子解释，再让它写回 不懂的名词.md。 一节课下来，你就有了一份属于自己的术语词典。Give each "unknown term" to AI one by one, ask it to explain in plain language with examples, then have it write back to 不懂的名词.md. By the end of the class, you'll have your own personal glossary.
💬 真实示例（一位同学真实输入的，直接抄、照着改）Real Example (from an actual student, copy and adapt)——核心是你只管 口述，开文件 / 写入 / 排版全交给 AI：The key is you just dictate, and AI handles opening files / writing / formatting:
  • 记一条心得（说完 AI 自动写进 我的心得.md）：Log a reflection (AI automatically writes it to 我的心得.md):

  • 记一个不懂的名词（AI 解释完自动存进 不懂的名词.md）：Log an unknown term (AI explains and saves it to 不懂的名词.md):
    「我不太懂什么叫做 dispatch。I don't quite understand what "dispatch" means.」
  • 不会的操作直接问步骤（问完顺手记进心得）：Ask directly about operations you don't know (then add them to your reflections):

  • 随时追加新想法：Add new thoughts anytime:
    「继续加入以下内容到我的心得：提示词可以按自己想法写，写不好就和 AI 讨论让它完善，写错了也能让它重做。Continue adding the following to my reflections: prompts can be written however you like — if not good enough, discuss with AI to refine it, and if it's wrong, you can have AI redo it.」

一句话窍门：你是指挥者——口述内容，建文件、写入、排版都交给 AI。One-line tip: you're the commander — dictate the content, and let AI handle creating files, writing, and formatting.

第 2 步Step 2 · 亲手做课堂项目Do the Class Project Yourself

只有自己做，才有真体会。You only truly learn by doing. 看录像、对着课程资料，自己在 课堂项目/ 里从头做一遍。Watch the recording, follow the materials, and redo everything from scratch in the 课堂项目/ folder.

  • 大胆自由发挥Be bold and experiment freely——AI 是能容错的，做错了不要紧。AI is forgiving, making mistakes is fine.
  • 遇到 AI 给的结果不太对时，直接告诉它哪里不对，让它纠正。这个「我说、它改」的来回，本身就是这门课最重要的技能。When AI's output isn't quite right, just tell it what's wrong and ask it to fix it. This back-and-forth of "I say, it revises" is itself the most important skill in this course.
  • 做的过程中，继续把心得、踩的坑、突然想通的点，记到 课程心得/我的心得.md 里，让 AI 帮你接着总结。As you work, keep logging your reflections, pitfalls you hit, and sudden realizations in 课程心得/我的心得.md, and ask AI to keep summarizing for you.

第 3 步Step 3 · 做作业 = 解决你自己的真实痛点Homework = Solve Your Real Problem

作业不是交差，是 用这节课的能力解决你日常工作里的一个痛点。项目可大可小。Homework isn't just a formality — it's about using this class's skills to solve a real pain point in your daily work. The project can be large or small.

先想痛点Start by identifying your pain point——它可能是：it might be:

  • 一件 单调重复 的事（每天导出报表、整理同样格式的文档）；或者Something monotonously repetitive (exporting reports daily, formatting documents the same way); or
  • 一堆 琐碎零散 的事（没有固定流程，但很烦）。A bunch of scattered small tasks (no fixed process, but annoying).

然后分两种情况：Then handle it in one of two ways:

你的情况Your situation怎么做What to do
痛点 已经有固定流程Pain point already has a fixed process试着把这个流程搬进 cowork，做成一个 agent / workflowTry moving that process into Cowork and turning it into an agent / workflow
痛点 还没成型Pain point doesn't have a clear process yet把「作业要求 + 你的痛点描述」一起给 AI，让它给你建议方案，保存成文件Give AI both "homework requirements + your pain point description" and ask it for a suggested solution, saved as a file

关键是迭代The key is iteration——拿到 AI 的建议后，别停。和它一起细化，就像我做 agent 时不断完善 prompt 那样：你提一点、它改一版、你再提……几轮下来，方案就成型了。全程继续写你的心得体会。Once you have AI's suggestions, don't stop there. Refine together, just like I kept improving prompts when building agents: you suggest something, it revises, you suggest again… after a few rounds, the solution takes shape. Keep writing your reflections throughout.

Prompt 示例Prompt Example：
「这是我的作业要求（作业要求.md），我日常工作的痛点是 ___。请帮我分析这个痛点适不适合用 AI/cowork 解决，给我 2-3 个方案，保存成 方案.md，并告诉我最简单能先跑起来的是哪个。」Here are my homework requirements (作业要求.md). My daily work pain point is ___. Please help me analyze whether this pain point is suitable for solving with AI/Cowork, give me 2-3 approaches, save them as 方案.md, and tell me which one is the simplest to get running first."

第 4 步Step 4 · 让 AI 帮你给我写一封邮件 = 提交作业Let AI Help You Write a Submission Email

最后，结合你这一路的 心得体会 和 课后作业，让 AI 帮你写一封邮件发给我。Finally, draw on your reflections and homework throughout this process, and ask AI to help you write an email to send me.

📌 关键说明Important note：这封邮件 就是你的作业提交——「作业要求」邮件里要的每一项内容，邮件里 一项都不能少。 让 AI 帮你写，不等于让 AI 帮你省。所以 prompt 里要把作业要求的每一项都点给 AI，让它逐项填满。This email is your homework submission — every item the "homework requirements" asks for must appear in the email, not a single one left out. Having AI help you write it doesn't mean having AI help you skip things. So make sure your prompt points out every requirement item so AI fills each one in.

作业要求里要交的（邮件正文必须全部覆盖）：What the homework requires (the email body must cover all of these):

  1. 工作流设计Workflow design——你设计了哪几个 Agent，各自职责、数据怎么从上一个传到下一个。which Agents you designed, their respective responsibilities, and how data flows from one to the next.
  2. 每个 Agent 的提示词Each Agent's prompts——角色 / 任务 / 输出格式三要素齐全，并写明输入格式和 OUTPUTS/ 输出路径。with all three elements complete: role / task / output format, plus input format and OUTPUTS/ path.
  3. 实际运行输出Actual run output——每个 Agent 真实跑出来的结果（不是占位符、不是「[略]」）。the real output from each Agent (not placeholders, not "[omitted]").
  4. 学习心得Learning reflections——≥200 字，且至少 3 条具体的个人观察（「这工具好用」不算，要写你真发现了什么）。≥200 words, with at least 3 specific personal observations ("this tool is useful" doesn't count — write what you actually discovered).

我想看到的不是完美，而是 你真的动手了、真的在想。困惑和卡点尤其欢迎——那正是下一节课我能帮你的地方。What I want to see is not perfection, but that you truly worked on it and truly thought about it. Confusion and roadblocks are especially welcome — those are exactly where I can help you in the next class.

一句话总结One-sentence summary
写心得 → 让 AI 总结 → 自己做 → 解决真痛点 → 反馈给我。Write reflections → Let AI summarize → Do it yourself → Solve a real pain point → Send it back.
每一步都亲手做，每一步都留下记录。这门课的能力，是「做」出来的，不是「听」出来的。Do every step yourself, and document every step. The skill in this course is built by doing, not listening.
