你在做什么What You're Doing

你带领一个 Claude AI 应用方向的学员社群：You're leading a student community focused on one Claude AI application direction:

  • 组织同方向学员定期碰面（Zoom）Organize regular Zoom meetups for students in your direction
  • 积累这个方向的可复用资源（GitHub）Build up reusable resources for this direction (GitHub)
  • 让愿意贡献的人有地方贡献，让只想用的人有东西可用Give contributors a place to share, and give learners something useful to take home
你不是内容创作者，不是老师，不是全职维护者。
你是这个方向的第一个探路者，负责把大家的经验汇聚起来。 You are not a content creator, teacher, or full-time maintainer.
You are the first pathfinder for this direction — your job is to bring people's experiences together.

每月具体怎么做Monthly Playbook

第 1 周：发通知Week 1: Send an announcement

在微信群发：Post in the WeChat group:

「[方向名] 本月 Zoom：[日期] [时间] PT，感兴趣的回复 +1，我发链接」 "[Direction name] this month's Zoom: [date] [time] PT — reply +1 if you're in and I'll send the link"

Austin 帮你建 Zoom 链接。Austin will set up the Zoom link for you.

第 2–3 周：开会Weeks 2–3: Hold the meeting

会议流程（60 分钟）：Meeting agenda (60 minutes):

时间Time 内容Content
10 分钟min 自我介绍 / 近况更新Introductions / recent updates
30 分钟min 主要议题：本月大家在做什么？遇到什么问题？Main topic: what's everyone working on? What problems came up?
15 分钟min 自愿演示：有做出东西的人 screen shareVolunteer demos: anyone with something to show does a screen share
5 分钟min 下次时间 + 有没有人想发起合作Schedule next meeting + anyone want to start a collaboration?

不需要准备 PPT。你主持，Austin 会参加前 1–2 次帮你暖场。No slides needed. You facilitate; Austin will join the first 1–2 sessions to help warm things up.

第 4 周：整理归档Week 4: Archive and organize

把会议里有价值的内容整理上传 GitHub：Organize and upload anything valuable from the meeting to GitHub:

  • 有人分享的 prompt → 放 prompts/Shared prompts → put in prompts/
  • skill 文件 → 放 skills/Skill files → put in skills/
  • 一篇总结 / 会议记录 → 放 笔记/Summary / meeting notes → put in 笔记/
  • 知识性内容 → 放 wiki/Knowledge content → put in wiki/

不会 git？直接发给 Austin，帮你传。Not comfortable with git? Send it to Austin and he'll upload it for you.

常见问题FAQ

Q：没人来怎么办？Q: What if no one shows up?
第一次不来很正常。持续发通知，做 2–3 次之后自然会有固定参与者。Austin 会帮你在大群里推。Low attendance on the first try is normal. Keep sending announcements — after 2–3 sessions you'll have regulars. Austin will promote it in the main group.
Q：我自己也在学，不够权威怎么办？Q: I'm still learning myself — am I qualified to lead?
组长不是专家，是组织者。你的价值是把大家聚在一起，不是讲课。A group leader is an organizer, not an expert. Your value is bringing people together, not teaching.
Q：大家都很忙，参与度低怎么办？Q: Everyone is busy and engagement is low — what do I do?
Zoom 录制下来，放到群里。没来的人可以看录像，下次会来。Record the Zoom and post it to the group. People who couldn't make it can watch the recording and will join next time.
Q：这个方向我不太熟怎么办？Q: I'm not very familiar with this direction — is that okay?
你感兴趣就行。Demo Day 上大家已经在做很多东西了，你帮他们找到彼此就够了。Being interested is enough. By Demo Day, everyone is already building things — you just help them find each other.
