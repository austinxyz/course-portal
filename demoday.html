教育税务知识库Education-tax KB
AI 给出的每一步都不能直接当真，尤其涉及具体税务计算时。"发现问题 → 当场要求修正 → 修正后的知识反哺回 wiki"的闭环，才是这套系统越用越强的原因，而不是每次从零开始。You can't take every AI step at face value — especially tax math. The loop of "spot it → fix it live → feed the correction back into the wiki" is what makes the system compound, instead of starting from zero each time.
H
Helen
教育税务知识库Education-tax KB
最有用的不是它告诉我"行"或"不行"，而是像一个外部审稿人，一条条挑出每个选题可能被质疑的地方再给补救办法。整套流程跑完，最大的收获是它能沉淀下来重复用——以后申请别的基金不用再从零摸索。The value wasn't a yes/no — it acted like an external reviewer, flagging every weak point in each topic and offering fixes. The real win: the whole workflow settles into something reusable, so the next grant isn't back to square one.
P
Pauline
科研申请工作流Research-grant workflow
工作流的"逻辑性"更多取决于人怎么拆解任务，AI 负责把设计好的逻辑稳定执行到位。对课题还是要有自己的洞见——AI 能帮你归纳整理，但不能替你做决定，数据来源也很重要。A workflow's logic depends on how the human breaks down the task; AI's job is to execute that logic reliably. You still need your own insight — AI can summarize, but it can't decide for you, and where the data comes from matters.
