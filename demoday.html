怎么报名How to sign up

三步，两分钟填完Three steps, two minutes
1

填表单Fill out the form

姓名、项目名、一句话描述、联系方式——不用写论文，项目还没做完也能报。Name, project name, one-line description, contact info — no essay needed, and it's fine if the project isn't finished yet.

2

Austin 筛阵容Austin curates the lineup

报名截止后，按项目多样性挑 12 位，确认后私信联系时段。没入选也不用担心——项目照样能上「学员作品」展示区。After the deadline, 12 speakers are picked for variety. Confirmed speakers get a DM about their slot — everyone else's project still appears in the student-work gallery.

3

上场Show up

9 分钟：1 分钟说问题，6 分钟直接跑给大家看，2 分钟讲一个坑或 insight。不用讲代码。9 minutes: 1 to state the problem, 6 to run it live, 2 for one pitfall or insight. No code walkthrough needed.
